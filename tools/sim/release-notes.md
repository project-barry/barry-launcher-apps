Try Barry Launcher apps on your computer, each in a window shaped like the AYN Thor's bottom screen, with their services and stand-in games. Nothing needs installing.

- **Windows** (64-bit): unzip **Barry-Simulator-Windows.zip** anywhere and double-click **Barry Simulator.exe**.
- **Linux**: download the **AppImage** for your computer (**x86_64** for most PCs, **aarch64** for ARM), allow it to run as a program (in its file properties, or `chmod +x`), and double-click it.
- **Mac**: build it once with `tools/sim/build-mac-app.sh`.

How to use it: [Barry Simulator](https://github.com/project-barry/barry-launcher-apps/wiki/Barry-Simulator).
